How to Play

Pick a difficulty and duration, then type the text shown. The clock starts on your first keypress.

  • Pink characters are correct; red-highlighted characters are mistakes.
  • Use Backspace to fix a mistake, or keep going.
  • WPM counts correct characters divided by five, per minute.
  • Accuracy is correct keystrokes out of all keystrokes.
  • Practice Timer runs for the time you choose, with new paragraphs arriving until it ends or you press Stop Test.
